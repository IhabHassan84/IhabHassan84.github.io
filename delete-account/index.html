Delete your Gym Rat account

This page explains how to delete your Gym Rat account (Gym Rat: Workout Planner, by Ihab Hassan (independent developer)) and what happens to your data.

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Gym Rat and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile > Account.
  3. Tap Delete account, type DELETE, and tap Delete forever.

You're signed out on all your phones straight away.

Option 2: without the app

If you've uninstalled the app or can't sign in, email ai.wise.au@gmail.com from the email address you use for Gym Rat, with the subject "Delete my account". If you sign in with Google, use your Google email address.

To protect your account, we only act on requests sent from the account's own email address. If you can't send from it, tell us and we'll ask you to confirm in another way. We'll reply and start the deletion within 30 days (usually much sooner).

What happens next

  • Straight away: your account is locked and you're signed out on every phone. Your data is hidden and can't be used.
  • For 30 days, you can change your mind: sign in to Gym Rat again and the deletion is cancelled, with everything as you left it.
  • After 30 days: your account and all its data are permanently deleted. This can't be undone.

What is deleted

Everything stored with your account:

  • your account (email address, password, and your Google sign-in link if you used one);
  • your profile: training preferences, body stats (weight, height, age, sex) and hidden exercises;
  • your weigh-ins;
  • your training plans, including earlier versions;
  • your workouts and every set you logged, including notes and effort ratings;
  • your deload and plan-change history.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Backups and server logs kept by our hosting provider (Supabase) may contain your data until they expire: daily backups up to 7 days on paid plans (none on the free plan), and short log retention set by Supabase. They are not used to restore deleted accounts.
  • On your phone: the app erases its copy of your data when you sign out, or the next time it opens after your account has been deleted. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on the phone.
  • We keep no other copies. We don't sell or share your data, so no other company holds a copy to delete.

Deleting the app doesn't delete your account. Use one of the options above.

Delete some data, keep your account

In the app you can delete individual sets, and clear any body stat in Profile > Units and body stats. For anything else, email ai.wise.au@gmail.com.
