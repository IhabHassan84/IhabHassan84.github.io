Gym Rat privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Gym Rat is a workout planner and training log. This policy explains what information Gym Rat collects, why, where it is kept, how long for, and how you can see, change or delete it. We wrote it in plain English. If anything is unclear, contact us at ai.wise.au@gmail.com.

Who we are

Gym Rat is provided by Ihab Hassan (independent developer). We are responsible for your information (the "controller" under data-protection laws such as the GDPR).

The short version

  • We collect what the app needs to build your plan and keep your training log: your email, your training preferences, your workouts, and any body stats you choose to enter.
  • No ads, no selling, no tracking. We don't sell or rent your information. We don't show ads. We don't use analytics or advertising SDKs, and the app doesn't read your advertising ID, location, contacts or photos.
  • Your data lives on your phone first and is backed up to our database so you can use it on another phone.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time. After a 30-day period in which you can change your mind, everything is permanently deleted.

Health-related information. Body stats, weigh-ins and workouts describe your body and physical activity, and the law may treat them as health data. We use them only to provide the app's features to you. Body stats and weigh-ins are optional; you can leave them blank or clear them in Profile.

How the app works with your data

  • On your phone first. Everything you do is saved in a database on your phone, so the app works without an internet connection. Your plan is generated on your phone.
  • Backed up to our database. When you're online, the app copies your changes to our database and fetches changes made on your other phones. Only your own account can read or change your data; our database enforces this for every row.
  • Personal records are calculated on your phone from your workouts and are not uploaded.
  • Settings that stay on your phone: theme, rest-timer behaviour, reminder settings and the random device ID.
  • Reminders (workout reminders, weekly summary, rest timer) are scheduled by the app on your phone. We don't send push notifications from a server, and no notification token is created or sent anywhere.
  • Exercise demos and fonts are built into the app. Watching a demo doesn't contact any server.

Deleting your account

  • In the app: Profile > Account > Delete account, type DELETE, and confirm.
  • Without the app: see <https://ihabhassan84.github.io/delete-account/> (the page in docs/launch/account-deletion-page.md).

What happens:

  1. You're signed out on every phone straight away, and your data is hidden. Nobody can use the account.
  2. For 30 days you can change your mind: sign in again and the deletion is cancelled. Everything is as you left it.
  3. After 30 days, your account and everything in it (profile, body stats, weigh-ins, plans, workouts and sets) are permanently deleted from our database, including your Google sign-in link if you used one. This can't be undone.
  4. Copies in Supabase's backups and logs disappear when those expire (see "How long we keep it").

Deleting the app from your phone doesn't delete your account.

Your rights and choices

  • See and correct: your profile, preferences, body stats, weigh-ins, plans and workouts are all visible and editable in the app.
  • Delete: individual sets in the app, or your whole account (above).
  • Clear optional data: you can clear any body stat at any time in Profile > Units and body stats.

Security

  • All traffic between the app and our database is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Our database lets each account read and write only its own rows.
  • Your sign-in session is stored in your phone's secure, hardware-backed storage (Android Keystore).
  • The app's data on your phone is excluded from Android cloud backups, so it isn't copied to other services.
  • Passwords are handled by our auth provider and stored only as a secure hash. We never see them.

No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach that affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

Children

Gym Rat is for people aged 16 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If you believe a child under 16 has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
